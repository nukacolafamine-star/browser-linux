Ordinary Linux programs.

This engine emulates an x86-64 PC in WebAssembly on your device and boots Debian 13 with a Wayland desktop and X11 support. Its disk is kept in this browser and downloads only the parts Linux reads. Your saved Linux/Wasm workspace stays in the original engine.
